Lesson 2 · The USB-C analogy

One connector, still subject to what each side supports

Before USB-C

Each device had its own cable: micro-USB, Lightning, proprietary.

With USB-C

One connector for chargers, displays, accessories. The device and cable must still support the feature.

Before MCP

Each AI application had its own custom integrations, rebuilt for every assistant.

With MCP

One server, reused by compatible clients, subject to their features and authentication requirements.

MCP workshop · Session 1Lesson 2
Click 1: hardware. Click 2: software. Insist on the limit: a USB-C cable does not make every device support every feature, and an MCP server does not make every client support every capability. 2 minutes.

CriterionFunction callingMCP
